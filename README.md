## Service areas

On Home and Service Areas, each card shows a real, freely licensed photo of that
area (from Wikimedia Commons, stored in `public/areas/`) with a small credit link,
and clicking the rounded location badge shows that area on an embedded Google Map
below the cards (same page, smooth scroll) with a "Back to locations" button. Area names, photos, alt text and photo credits are maintained in
`data/serviceAreas.ts`. When replacing a photo, keep the credit up to date.
